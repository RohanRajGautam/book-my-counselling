We're starting this blog to share what we've learned from years of helping students and professionals make confident decisions about their education and careers.

## What you'll find here

- Practical advice on applications, scholarships, and studying abroad
- Honest stories from students in different countries and programs
- Mentor perspectives on what makes a great mentee
- Updates from the BYC team

## How to read it

Posts are short on purpose. We try to write the way we'd explain something to a friend — concrete, specific, and skippable when you only have a minute.

Thanks for reading. If there's a topic you'd like us to cover, let us know.
